Each <name>-<version>.rktd is derived by gen-modelinfo.rkt (`racket model/gen-modelinfo.rkt in out`);
models.rktd lists the shipped models and their representations. QI-Core and US Core are mapping
models (targetUrl http://hl7.org/fhir): their ELM reads FHIR through each element's target mapping,
so they have no representation of their own. qdm.representation.rktd is written here: how QDM data
looks in the cqm-models JSON (Bonnie, Cypress).
